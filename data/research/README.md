# Sources, kept rather than re-fetched

What a rule in this product rests on, as it was read. A citation that needs a download to check is
a citation nobody checks, and the two documents here are both served by the National Board of
Revenue in ways that make re-fetching unreliable: the Act refuses a plain request and answers only
something browser-shaped, and the form's own text layer is legacy Bengali that extracts as
fragments.

|---|---|---|---|

| `vat_sd_act_2012_nbr_english.txt` | The same, as `pdftotext -layout` renders it, so a section can be found by searching | extracted here | 13 September 2026 |
